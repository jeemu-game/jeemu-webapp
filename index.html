Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Jeemu and how does it work?

Jeemu combines 45 precision-milled beechwood blocks with internal NFC tags and a dedicated smart game box. You pull a block from the tower, tap it onto the dock, and the dock immediately identifies the block and displays dynamic questions, challenges, or rules on its OLED screen based on your chosen game mode.

Do we need our phones out while playing?

No! Once you choose or create a game configuration in the mobile app, you beam it to the Jeemu smart dock via Bluetooth. After that, put all smartphones away. The Jeemu hardware runs the entire game independently.

How does the $1 VIP Reservation deposit work?

By putting down a small refundable $1 deposit, you lock in the guaranteed lowest price of $89 (a $70 discount off the $159 MSRP), plus a free gold-stamped Founder's Edition block and Wave 1 priority delivery. If you change your mind for any reason before Kickstarter ends, we refund 100% of your $1 deposit immediately.

Can I make my own game modes with custom questions?

Yes! You can manually type prompts for every block or use our AI Generator to build complete themed game packs in seconds (e.g. "Law School Graduation Party", "Couple's Anniversary", or "Late Night Horror Trivia").
